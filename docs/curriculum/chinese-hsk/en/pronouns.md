Pronouns are the backbone of simple sentences: **我是…**, **你叫什么名字？**

## Notes

- **他** and **她** share the same pinyin *tā* — tone and context matter; characters differ.
- **们** turns singular pronouns into plural (**我们**, **你们**).
- **名字** is “name”; **人** is “person”.
