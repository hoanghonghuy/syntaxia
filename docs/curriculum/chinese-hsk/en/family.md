Family vocabulary is early Band 1 — useful for introductions: **我家有…**

## Notes

- Sibling terms encode age relative to you (**哥哥** vs **弟弟**).
- **家** covers both “home” and “family” depending on context.

## Your turn

Choose **妈妈**.
